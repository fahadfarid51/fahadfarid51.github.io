Deep Learning-Based Digital Twining Models for Inter System Behavior and Health Assessment of Combat Aircraft Systems

Agrawal, A., Farid, F., Prabhu, A., Sudhakar, V., Vyas, N.
SAE Technical Paper · AeroCON 2024, Bangalore · Track Best Paper, Innovation

  • Neural networks built per aircraft system from onboard sensor data, with feature selection driven by domain knowledge and correlation analysis.
  • Individual system outputs combined into an overall health assessment, enabling fault isolation at system level from subtle deviations in behaviour.
  • Groundwork for condition-based maintenance: real-time monitoring, anomaly detection and root cause analysis.

Enhancement of Natural Convection Heat Transfer in Cylindrical Enclosure with Internal Heat Source

Gautam, S., Khan, M. J., Khan, A., Sharma, V., Farid, F., Sharma, A. K.
Recent Advances in Manufacturing, Automation, Design and Energy Technologies · Lecture Notes in Mechanical Engineering, Springer Singapore · pp. 983–992

  • Studied natural convection heat transfer in a cylindrical enclosure with an internal heat source — the problem behind cooling nuclear reactors and electronic equipment.
  • Two fluids at very different Prandtl numbers, air and liquid sodium, solved in the laminar regime with a finite volume CFD solver.
  • Five chimney configurations compared: for air, any plate with an opening improves heat removal; for liquid sodium, the funnel configuration performed best.
